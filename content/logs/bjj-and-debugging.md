There's a pattern I keep noticing between rolling on the mat and debugging firmware.

In BJJ, when you're stuck in a bad position, the instinct is to explode out with
raw force. It almost never works. What works is: stop, breathe, identify the
specific point of control your opponent has, and address *that*  not the
general situation.

Debugging is identical. When a system is misbehaving, the instinct is to change
multiple things at once. It almost never works. What works is: stop, form a
hypothesis about the *single* root cause, isolate it, test it.

Both disciplines punish impatience and reward systematic thinking.

The oscilloscope is the GoPro replay of embedded debugging. Watch the signal.
The answer is always in the signal.
